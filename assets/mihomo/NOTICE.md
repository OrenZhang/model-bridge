# Mihomo

Model Bridge release installers include an unmodified official Mihomo v1.19.32 archive
for their target platform. The archive's SHA-256 is pinned in
`src/network/mihomo-artifacts.json` and verified both during packaging and installation.

Mihomo is licensed under GPL-3.0. Its license is included in this directory.
Corresponding source for this exact version is freely available from:
https://github.com/MetaCubeX/mihomo/tree/v1.19.32
https://github.com/MetaCubeX/mihomo/archive/refs/tags/v1.19.32.tar.gz

Model Bridge does not modify Mihomo or import subscription rules, DNS or TUN settings.
